FAQ

Got Questions? We've Got Answers.

Everything you need to know about booking with Stackly.

How do I book a mechanic?
Enter your car details and postcode, choose a service, pick a time slot, and confirm. It takes less than 2 minutes!
Are your mechanics qualified?
Yes. Every mechanic on our platform is vetted, fully insured, and holds relevant industry qualifications (IMI, City & Guilds, or equivalent).
What's included in the price?
The price shown includes all parts and labour. There are no hidden fees or call-out charges – what you see is exactly what you pay.
Do you offer a guarantee?
Absolutely. All work comes with a 12-month / 12,000-mile parts and labour guarantee for your complete peace of mind.
Can the mechanic come to my home?
Yes! Our mobile mechanics can come to your home, workplace, or any location with safe, level ground and adequate space to work.
What if I need to cancel or reschedule?
You can cancel or reschedule for free up to 24 hours before your appointment. Just log in to your account or contact our support team.

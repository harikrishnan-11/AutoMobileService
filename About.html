Our Story

From a garage frustration to a national platform

ClickMechanic started in 2012 when co-founders Andrew and Harry spent three weeks trying to get a fair quote for a simple clutch repair. The process was opaque, stressful, and expensive.

They believed there was a better way. By connecting drivers directly with local, vetted mechanics — with full pricing shown upfront — they could remove the anxiety from car ownership altogether.

"We didn't just want to build a booking tool. We wanted to restore trust between drivers and mechanics — one repair at a time."

More than a decade later, ClickMechanic is trusted by over 500,000 drivers and 14,000 mechanics across the UK. We've completed millions of jobs and earned an industry-leading 98% satisfaction score.

What We Stand For

Our Mission & Core Values

Everything we build, every decision we make, flows from these five principles.

01

Radical Transparency

We show the full price before you book — parts, labour, and everything in between. No hidden charges, ever.

02

Trust at Every Step

Every mechanic on our platform is background-checked, insured, and continuously reviewed by real customers.

03

Speed & Convenience

From booking to completion, we respect your time. Next-day slots, mobile mechanics, zero waiting rooms.

04

Fair Earnings for Mechanics

We believe mechanics deserve great pay and flexibility. Our platform is built to help them thrive.

05

Sustainable Practices

We're committed to reducing the environmental impact of car servicing through smarter logistics and eco-conscious partners.

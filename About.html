Our Story

From a garage frustration to a national platform

Stackly started in 2012 when co-founders Andrew and Harry spent three weeks trying to get a fair quote for a simple clutch repair. The process was opaque, stressful, and expensive.

They believed there was a better way. By connecting drivers directly with local, vetted mechanics — with full pricing shown upfront — they could remove the anxiety from car ownership altogether.

"We didn't just want to build a booking tool. We wanted to restore trust between drivers and mechanics — one repair at a time."

More than a decade later, Stackly is trusted by over 500,000 drivers and 14,000 mechanics across the Chennai. We've completed millions of jobs and earned an industry-leading 98% satisfaction score.

01

Radical Transparency

We show the full price before you book — parts, labour, and everything in between. No hidden charges, ever.
